📘 Sign convention & physical meaning

Coordinate direction: upward is positive (+y); downward is negative (−y).

Height above ground: never negative before impact. It decreases while falling, even though the magnitude of the downward velocity increases.

Displacement: Δy = y − y₀ (negative for net downward movement).

Assumption: constant gravity, no air resistance, and collision at ground level.
